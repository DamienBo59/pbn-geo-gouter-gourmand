My name is Marion Kieffer. I have been baking afternoon treats at home for about ten years, first for my children, then because I grew to love it.

I am not a pastry chef. I work in an ordinary kitchen, with a cheap electric oven that runs far too hot underneath, one decent tin and an ageing scale. That is exactly where this site comes from.

## Why this blog exists

For two years, I got almost everything wrong. Soggy tart bases, cakes raw in the middle and burnt underneath, creams that split. For a long time I assumed I was the problem.

In fact, most recipes leave out what matters. They give a temperature without mentioning it assumes a well-calibrated fan oven. They say "leave to rest" without saying how long, or why. They assume equipment they never name.

Here I write the opposite: what the recipe does not tell you, what happens when it goes wrong, and how to manage with the equipment you already own.

## What you will find

Everyday cakes, tarts and their base pastries, small bakes, creams and technique. And a whole section devoted to baking **without**: without an oven, without butter, without sugar, without eggs. Not for dietary reasons, that is not my subject, but because an empty cupboard or a broken oven should not be the end of the story.

## How I work

I test at home, in ordinary conditions. When a recipe only works with particular equipment, I say so. When I have not verified something, I say that too.
